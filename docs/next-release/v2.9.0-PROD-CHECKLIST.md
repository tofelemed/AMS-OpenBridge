## 8. Rollback

**Triggers** (decide before the window):
- a cpm-17..21 script still fails after one re-run;
- cplm-api not healthy 10 min after step 5, or in a restart loop;
- fewer than 8 Flink jobs RUNNING 10 min after 04b, or a job restarting repeatedly;
- the delta `verify` is not `VERIFY OK` after one re-run;
- `root.hdpe.cpm` last pv not advancing 10 min after T_FLIP;
- login fails, or several sidebar pages show error panels;
- `df -h /` below 15 GB at any point.

**How:**
- [ ] **Images:** from `/tmp/pre-$R-images.txt` (cplm-api, ams-frontend, ams-api, traverse-auth-service, audit-service, asset-model,
      traverse-ingestion-service, the flink image **and the previous jar**), then the full Flink swap again (cancel, rm -f, deploy.sh, 04b -> 7 jobs).
- [ ] **Schema** (newest first; roll the cplm-api image back BEFORE cpm-21 / cpm-20): cpm-21 (charts back to 6 h), cpm-20b, cpm-20
      (EVALUATE runs deleted), cpm-19 (archive lost), cpm-18 (3-year history lost), cpm-17 (cplm-api stopped), then the prune cron back:
      `sudo cp migration/cron.d/cpa-prune-short-features /etc/cron.d/ && sudo chmod 644 /etc/cron.d/cpa-prune-short-features`.
- [ ] **IoTDB** (old tree intact until the 7-day delete), then copy back what was written after T_FLIP with `--layout v2-back`
      (runbook "Rollback"; switch the writers back BEFORE the copy-back):
  - full rollback: remove all four `.env` lines, load the previous images, `deploy.sh --prod`;
  - IoTDB only (CHG-102 images kept): remove only `LOOP_ROOT_PREFIX`, `IOTDB_LOOP_DB`, `IOTDB_LAYOUT` (keep `PLANT_TIME_ZONE=IR`:
    without it boundary evidence and KPI daily shift to UTC), load the **previous ams-frontend** image (the prefix is baked in),
    `deploy.sh --prod`, then cancel the On-Demand Evaluation job and run `04b` so it picks the old prefix up.
  - Keep `root.hdpe.cpm` for 30 d after a rollback (archived sets read from it). A cplm-api older than `125e6865` cannot read
    `cpax2` archive rows.
- [ ] **Redesign:** no data to undo; the previous images are enough.
- [ ] **Topics:** leave the two evaluation topics (never delete topics on the shared broker).
